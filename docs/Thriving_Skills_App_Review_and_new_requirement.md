# **Mobile App Review** 

Corrections & change requests for the delivered build 

**Build reviewed:** Version 4.0.2.3 **Review date:** 17 November 2026 

Thank you for delivering the first build. After reviewing it, our team has collected the corrections below. Each section shows what we found, the change we need, and screenshots: **red** labels mark the current build and **green** labels mark the layout or behaviour we expect. 

### **Summary of changes** 

|**#**|**Area**|**Change requested**|
|---|---|---|
|**1**|**Splash screen & login**|Add “Thriving Skills” wordmark and tagline; login mandatory|
|**2**|**Home page**|Declutter; follow the lighter reference layout|
|**3**|**Home – category carousels**|Per-category course rows that auto-scroll|
|**4**|**All Courses – categories**|Department-wise categories that jump to course rows|
|**5**|**Bundle courses**|Add-to-cart adds all 5 courses; all unlock after payment|
|**6**|**Reviews & downloads**|Reviews for enrolled learners only; remove video download|
|**7**|**Video player**|Smoother playback; fix duplicated duration bar|
|**8**|**My Achievements**|Certificates section with Facebook / LinkedIn sharing|
